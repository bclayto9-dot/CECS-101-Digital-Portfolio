SMART Goals Activity
Vague intentions do not produce results. This activity moves you from intention to commitment — from "I hope to" into "I will, by when, measured how." You will write a minimum of 5 SMART Goals across academic, career, and personal development categories.

Step 1 — Review Your POV Statement
Reread your POV Statement. Your goals should move you in the direction defined by your POV Statement. If they do not connect, something is misaligned.

Step 2 — Brainstorm Goal Areas
Before writing formal SMART goals, brainstorm freely. For each category below, list 3–4 things you want to accomplish in the next 1–4 years. Do not filter yourself in this step — just generate ideas.

Academic (courses, GPA, relationships with faculty, study habits...)
Career (internships, skills, certifications, networking...)
Personal Development (campus involvement, health, community, leadership...)
Step 3 — Select and Write 5 SMART Goals
Choose at least 5 goals from your brainstorm — at minimum, include:

2 Academic Goals (at least one short-term and one mid-term)

Improve my grades in my finance and accounting classes
Complete courses that build useful finance skills

2 Career Goals (at least one short-term and one mid-term)

Get a finance or accounting internship
Network with people working in finance

1 Personal Development Goal

Improve my communication skills

For each goal, complete the following SMART evaluation.

Goal 1 (Academic — Short-Term)
Goal Statement: [Write a clear, complete goal statement] I will earn at least a B in each of my finance and accounting-related classes this semester.

S — Specific:
I will focus on improving my performance in my finance and accounting classes by studying consistently and staying caught up with assignments.

M — Measurable:
I will track my grades throughout the semester and aim to finish each class with at least an 80%.

A — Achievable:
This is realistic because I can improve my grades by keeping up with assignments, studying before exams, and asking for help when I need it.

R — Relevant:
Doing well in these classes will help me build the knowledge I need for a career in finance.

T — Time-Bound:
I will achieve this by the end of the Fall 2026 semester.

Immediate Next Action: What is one concrete step I can take THIS WEEK toward this goal? This week, I will review my current grades and make a study plan for each class.
