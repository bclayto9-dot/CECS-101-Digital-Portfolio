Career 1
Career Title: Financial Analyst

Brief Description (what does this person do day to day?): Financial analysts examine financial data and help businesses or individuals make decisions about money and investments. 
Their day-to-day work can include reviewing financial statements, researching economic and business trends, creating financial models, evaluating investments, and making recommendations based on their findings.

Typical Education Required: A bachelor's degree is typically required. Though not always.

Key Skills Required (list at least 6):

Financial analysis
Critical thinking
Analytical and quantitative skills
Communication
Attention to detail
Computer skills

Key Knowledge Areas Required: Finance, accounting, economics, investments, financial markets, statistics, and business management.

Median Annual Salary: $102,740

Job Growth Outlook (growing, stable, declining?): Growing. Employment of financial analysts is projected to grow 7% from 2025 to 2035.

Typical Career Path (how do people enter and advance in this field?): A person can enter the field after earning a bachelor's degree in finance or a related area. Many begin as junior or entry-level financial analysts. 
With experience, they can advance to senior financial analyst positions and eventually move into management positions such as finance manager or financial manager.

3 specific courses or experiences that would build skills for this career:

Financial Management
Financial Statement Analysis
An internship in corporate finance, banking, or financial analysis

Career 2
Career Title: Financial Manager9

Brief Description (what does this person do day to day?): Financial managers are responsible for the financial health of an organization.
They prepare and review financial reports, develop financial plans, analyze business performance, oversee investments and budgets, and help management make long-term financial decisions.

Typical Education Required: A bachelor's degree in finance, accounting, economics, or a related business field is typically required. 
Financial managers also usually need five or more years of experience in another financial or business occupation.

Key Skills Required (list at least 6):

Financial analysis
Leadership
Decision-making
Communication
Strategic planning
Attention to detail

Key Knowledge Areas Required: Finance, accounting, budgeting, financial reporting, economics, investments, risk management, and business strategy.

Median Annual Salary: $166,570

Job Growth Outlook (growing, stable, declining?): Growing. Employment of financial managers is projected to grow 10% from 2025 to 2035

Typical Career Path (how do people enter and advance in this field?): Financial managers usually do not enter the position immediately after college.
A person may begin as a financial analyst, accountant, or another financial professional. After gaining several years of experience, they may become a senior analyst or supervisor before advancing into financial management.
From there, opportunities can include positions such as finance director, controller, or chief financial officer.

3 specific courses or experiences that would build skills for this career:

Financial Management
Financial Statement Analysis
An accounting or corporate finance internship
